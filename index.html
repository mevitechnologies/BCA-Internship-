Yes. One-month programs start with ₹1,000 and the balance is due in the final week. Three-month programs are ₹2,000 per month.

For one month, a final project costs ₹1,000 extra. For three months, the final project and report are included.

No. Hardware pricing is extra and depends on the selected components.

Our learning roadmaps begin with foundations and progress to practical applications. Regular practice is important.

No. Career roles are illustrative pathways. Training develops skills but does not guarantee employment.
